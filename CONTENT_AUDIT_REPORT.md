# Content Audit Report - Renovision Clone Website

## Executive Summary
Comprehensive audit of all 20+ pages to identify duplicate content and ensure unique, SEO-optimized heading structures.

## Key Findings

### CRITICAL DUPLICATES IDENTIFIED

#### 1. **Location Pages (Tacoma, Gig Harbor, Lakewood, Puyallup)**
**Issue:** Nearly identical content structure and headings
- H1: "General Contractor in [CITY], WA" ✓ (Location-specific)
- H2: "Why Choose Renovision for Your [CITY] Home" ✓ (Location-specific)
- H2: "Services We Offer in [CITY]" ✓ (Location-specific)
- **DUPLICATE H2:** "Serving [COUNTY] & Surrounding Areas" (Same structure, different counties)
- **DUPLICATE H2:** "Get in Touch" (Identical across all 5 location pages)
- **DUPLICATE H3:** "Design-Build Model", "In-House Crews", "AI 3D Visualization", "Fixed-Price Transparency", "Licensed & Bonded" (Identical across all location pages)

**Impact:** High - Search engines may penalize for duplicate content
**Priority:** HIGH

#### 2. **Service Pages (Kitchen, Bathroom, Whole Home, ADU, Basement, Deck & Patio, New Construction)**
**Issue:** Templated content with minimal differentiation
- H1: "[Service Name] Remodeling" ✓ (Service-specific)
- **DUPLICATE H2:** "Why Choose Renovision for Your [Service] Project" (Same structure)
- **DUPLICATE H2:** "What's Included in Your [Service] Project" (Same structure)
- **DUPLICATE H2:** "The [Service] Remodeling Process" (Same structure)
- **DUPLICATE H3:** "Design & Planning", "Materials & Permits", "Construction", "Final Walkthrough" (Identical process steps)
- **DUPLICATE H3:** "Licensed & Bonded", "Transparent Pricing", "In-House Crews" (Identical benefits)

**Impact:** High - Thin content across 7 pages
**Priority:** HIGH

#### 3. **Company Pages (About Us, Services Overview)**
**Issue:** Overlapping messaging
- Both pages mention "design-build approach"
- Both pages list all 7 services
- Similar "Why Choose" messaging

**Impact:** Medium
**Priority:** MEDIUM

#### 4. **Global Duplicates**
- **Footer:** "Get in Touch", "Office Location" sections appear on every location page
- **Navbar:** Services dropdown identical across all pages
- **Contact Section:** Identical phone, email, hours on all location pages

**Impact:** Expected (global elements) - No action needed
